FAQ

Questions, answered

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The consultation, survey and first design visual are completely free with no obligation to go ahead.

How much does a shop sign cost?

Most shop front signs fall between £1,000 and £5,000 depending on size, materials and illumination. You'll get a fixed, itemised quote after your consultation.

Do I need planning permission?

Illuminated signs and some larger signs often need advertisement consent. We'll tell you during the consultation and can handle the application for you.

How long does it take?

Typical turnaround is 7–14 working days from design sign-off, plus any time needed for planning approval.

Which areas do you cover?

All London boroughs and the surrounding Home Counties. Video consultations are available UK-wide.
